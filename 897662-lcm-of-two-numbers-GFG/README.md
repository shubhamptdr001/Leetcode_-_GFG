# [LCM of Two Numbers](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/lcm-of-two-numbers/1)
## Easy
Given two positive integers a and b, find the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of the two numbers.Examples:Input: a = 12, b = 18Output: 36Explanation: LCM of 12 and 18 is 36Input: a = 5, b = 11Output: 55Explanation: LCM of 5 and 11 is 55
